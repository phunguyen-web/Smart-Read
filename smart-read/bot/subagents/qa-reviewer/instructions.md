- Exercise rotate 90/180, reverse order, page numbers, odd/even select, delete, duplicate, extract, keep selection, split to ZIP, insert blank, forget local copy, reorder (including start/end), merge, overlay, persisted undo after reload, TXT/MD/HTML import, and each export format including Markdown.
- Confirm the 20 MB limit and empty-file errors.
- Note keyboard access on the editor controls.
- List what passed, what failed, and the smallest fix.

- Expand product scope.
- Rewrite features unless a check is blocked.
